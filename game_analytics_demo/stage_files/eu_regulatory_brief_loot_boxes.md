# EU Regulatory Brief: Loot Boxes, In-Game Monetization and Age Ratings

> **Illustrative demo content.** This is a fictional internal compliance brief. The regulatory summary is simplified and is **not legal advice**. The risk rubric is an internal convention.

- **To:** Legal and Compliance, Live-Ops, Portfolio Strategy
- **Scope:** Sony live-service and monetized titles sold in the EU/EEA

## 1. Regulatory landscape (summary)

| Jurisdiction or instrument | Stance (simplified) | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Belgium** (Gaming Commission, 2018) | Paid random-reward loot boxes are treated as gambling and are effectively banned. | Any paid loot box mechanic cannot ship in Belgium. |
| **Netherlands** | Earlier enforcement against transferable loot boxes. The government is pursuing an EU-level ban. | High scrutiny of loot boxes and tradable items. |
| **EU Digital Services Act (DSA)** | Duties on protecting minors and avoiding dark patterns on online platforms. | Online-only games with minors in their audience face transparency and design obligations. |
| **EU consumer protection (CPC Network)** | Enforcement on in-game currency pricing transparency and pressure selling. | Microtransactions using virtual currency must show real-money prices. |
| **PEGI** | Age ratings, including the "In-game purchases" and "includes random items" descriptors. | A PEGI 18 rating combined with monetization draws regulator and media attention. |

## 2. Internal EU risk rubric

This rubric is the single source of truth for EU regulatory risk tiers. Apply the tiers in order; a game takes the first tier it meets.

| Tier | Condition |
|---|---|
| **HIGH** | The game is rated PEGI 18, **or** it monetizes through paid loot boxes and is available on mobile (Android or Apple). |
| **ELEVATED** | A live-service game (requires an online connection **and** has recurring monetization such as a battle pass, microtransactions, a subscription or loot boxes) **and** is rated below PEGI 18. A rating below 18 means minors can be in the audience, which brings DSA and consumer-protection exposure. |
| **WATCH** | Not live-service and not HIGH, but sold with paid DLC. Exposure is limited to advertising and marketing guidance. (A PEGI 18 game is HIGH even without recurring monetization; in that case its exposure is mainly reputational.) |
| **LOW** | Everything else. |

### Why each factor matters

- **Online required:** brings DSA platform duties and live data collection.
- **Microtransactions or virtual currency:** consumer-protection pricing rules apply.
- **Loot boxes:** classified as gambling in Belgium, with growing pressure across the EU.
- **PEGI below 18:** minors are in the audience, which is the main enforcement focus.
- **Mobile availability:** the largest minor audience and the most regulator attention.

## 3. Required mitigations by tier

| Tier | Required actions |
|---|---|
| HIGH | Legal review before any EU content update. Disable paid random rewards in Belgium. Publish drop rates. |
| ELEVATED | Show real-money pricing next to virtual currency. Turn on spending limits and parental controls by default. Avoid time-limited pressure offers aimed at under-18 accounts. Review annually. |
| WATCH | Confirm DLC marketing follows PEGI advertising guidance. |
| LOW | No action. |

## 4. What this brief does not cover

This brief contains no fines, enforcement probabilities, country-level revenue or player-age demographics. Questions that need them should say so.
