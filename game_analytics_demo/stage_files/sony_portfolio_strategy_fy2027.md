# Sony Interactive Entertainment: FY2027 Portfolio Strategy Memo

> **Illustrative demo content.** This memo is a fictional internal strategy document. The strategic tiers, priorities and targets are invented for illustration.

- **To:** Portfolio Strategy, Franchise Leads, Studio Heads
- **From:** Portfolio Strategy Office
- **Fiscal year:** FY2027 = calendar year 2027 (January 1 to December 31)

## 1. Who "we" are

"Our portfolio" means the franchises published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. Ownership is defined by the publisher of the franchise, not by which studios we have acquired. Franchises from all other publishers are competitors.

## 2. Franchise strategic tiers

Every Sony franchise has exactly one strategic tier for FY2027. When we refer to "core", "growth" or "harvest" franchises, we mean the assignments below. Tiers are a strategic decision; they are not derived from revenue or the number of titles in a franchise.

**FY2027 tier assignments:** God of War is Core. Gran Turismo is Core. Marvel's Spider-Man is Growth. Bloodborne is Growth. Until Dawn is Harvest.

### God of War: Core franchise

God of War is our flagship narrative action IP. Protect its release cadence and maximize its reach on PC. No other first-party release should crowd its launch window.

### Gran Turismo: Core franchise

Gran Turismo is our flagship racing IP and includes the only live-service title in the portfolio, Gran Turismo 7. Sustain live-operations revenue and keep its monetization compliant.

### Marvel's Spider-Man: Growth franchise

Marvel's Spider-Man is our licensed open-world action IP. Grow its audience share and expand it to PC. It is a Growth franchise, not a Core one: it competes with God of War for the same players, so it must be positioned clearly apart from God of War.

### Bloodborne: Growth franchise

Bloodborne is a single-title Soulslike IP with strong brand equity. Assess a sequel or remaster, and evaluate it as our vehicle for entering the Soulslike genre.

### Until Dawn: Harvest franchise

Until Dawn is our narrative horror IP. Minimize new investment and monetize the catalog through remasters and PC releases. It is a candidate for strategic review.

### Tier definitions

- **Core:** our largest, most proven IPs. They receive guaranteed investment, launch-window protection and first claim on marketing.
- **Growth:** IPs we intend to scale. They receive incremental investment, provided they don't cannibalize a Core franchise.
- **Harvest:** IPs maintained for catalog revenue. No new full-scale productions without a strategy review.

## 3. Key business metrics

**Audience overlap** is the share of players two titles have in common, from 0% to 100%. We group it into three bands:

| Band | Overlap | Share of all title pairs |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | 60% or more | Top ~3% of pairs (about the 97th percentile and above) |
| **Medium** | 30% to 59% | Next ~7% (roughly the 90th to 97th percentile) |
| **Low** | Below 30% | Bottom ~90% of pairs |

Overlap is measured in both directions: a pair counts if either title overlaps the other.

**Genre market status** describes how crowded a genre combination is:

| Status | Number of games in the market |
|---|---|
| **Whitespace** | None |
| **Underserved** | 1 to 2 |
| **Served** | 3 or more |

## 4. FY2027 strategic priorities

1. **Reduce internal audience cannibalization.** We do not want two first-party franchises competing for the same players. Where a Growth franchise has High audience overlap (60% or more) with another of our franchises, that is a priority problem, and the Growth franchise repositions, not the Core one.
2. **Protect Core launch windows.** No Growth or Harvest release should ship in the same quarter as a Core release when the two titles have 30% or more audience overlap (Medium or High band, roughly the top 10% of pairs).
3. **Enter one underserved genre.** Target a genre combination that is Whitespace (no games) or Underserved (1 to 2 games). Keep in mind that whitespace signals low supply of games, not proven player demand. The preferred vehicle is a Growth franchise, with Bloodborne first in line.
4. **Expand PC reach for Core and Growth IPs.**
5. **Keep live-service monetization compliant**, especially in the EU. See the EU Regulatory Brief on loot boxes and in-game monetization.

## 5. Guidance for common questions

| Question | How we approach it |
|---|---|
| Which Growth franchises overlap with each other? | Look only at Growth-tier franchises and compare their audience overlap. Cite the overlap percentage, its band and the tier. |
| Which Growth franchise threatens a Core franchise? | Look for Growth vs. Core pairs with High audience overlap (60% or more). Recommend repositioning the Growth IP (priority 1). |
| What should we do with Harvest franchises? | Report their catalog revenue and recommend a strategic review, not new production. |
| What are the top revenue earners in our portfolio? | Rank our top five titles by lifetime revenue. Tiers don't change this ranking. |

## 6. What this memo does not cover

This memo contains no budgets, headcount, development timelines or market-size figures. Questions that need them should say so rather than infer them from the tiers.
